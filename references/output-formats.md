# Output formats

Follow `output.language` for spelling (e.g. British English). Keep prose short; the user wants to act, not read.

## Opening line

Every job search reply opens with the settings used:

> **Running [BACKFILL | DAILY] · [track] · [scope] · company batch [N | all]**

Add "(couldn't read search history, defaulting to BACKFILL)" when relevant.

Then one line on exclusions: "Skipped X roles already applied to / rejected / not interested."

## 5a. Ranked shortlist (GO only: verified and 7+)

Summary table first:

| # | Company | Role | Location | Source | Fit | GO/SKIP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Company | Role title | City · Hybrid | Greenhouse | 8/10 | ✅ GO |

Then a detail block for each GO role:

> **[Company] — [Role title]**
> Fit: X/10 | [Step up / Lateral / Step down] | ✅ GO
> Link: [direct careers or ATS URL]
> Posted: [date or "X days ago"]
> Source: [where it was found]
> Verified: [date] on [careers page / ATS page], showing the job description.
> 🔁 Re-apply: [only if the tracker has an older application here — when, how far you got, the old note, and what to do differently]
> [2–3 sentences: what matches, what to watch for, salary vs effective floor, visa note if relocation, what transfers if new industry]

If the **Verified** line can't be filled in honestly, the role is not GO. Move it to 5a-bis.

## 5a-bis. Leads to check yourself (unverified)

| Company | Role | Why unverified | Best available link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company | Role | Careers page needs JavaScript, couldn't confirm | https://… (aggregator link, may be stale) |

Fit scores here are capped at 6/10.

## 5b. Recruiters actively hiring

| Recruiter | Company | Role described | Post date | LinkedIn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Company | Senior … (Hybrid, City) | 3 days ago | linkedin.com/in/… |

For each: flag posts older than 14 days (likely filled), suggest a one-line outreach message, and advise reading the post carefully before messaging.

## Daily briefing (DAILY sessions)

> **TODAY'S BRIEFING — [date]**
> Settings: DAILY · [track] · [scope] · company batch [N]
> New roles found: X (Y skipped as already applied)
> Strong fits (7+): list with link, verdict, posting date, source
> Leads to check yourself: X
> Recruiters actively posting: X (see table)
> Recommended action: which to prioritise today

If nothing new turned up, say so plainly. Don't pad.

## Closing lines

Always end with:
1. "Which of these would you like a full fit check on?"
2. "Anything to skip next time? Just tell me."
3. The scope-expansion offer: "Want me to also check [next scope] this session?"

## Mode 1 (fit check) layout

1. **Fit: X/10** — one-line verdict
2. **Live check:** verified / not live / couldn't confirm
3. **ATS keywords to include:** comma-separated, exact wording from the JD
4. **Tailor these CV sections:** bullet list naming the section and the change
5. **Red flags:** bullet list, or "None found"
6. **Recommendation:** ✅ GO / ❌ NO-GO, with one sentence why
